Titan changes implement an adapter-local render-to-capture PCM queue, remove
synthetic capture tones and recording-to-disk, remove fictional hardware nodes,
bound allocations and notification lists, harden stream lifecycle and format
validation, and provide branded INF endpoint properties. No timer or remote
license activation is currently enforced. Microsoft signing and broader runtime
compatibility qualification are separate steps.
